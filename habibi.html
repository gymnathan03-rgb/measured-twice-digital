Habibi

Habibi is a personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows computer and phone. It was built by Measured Twice Digital for its own use. It is not a public product and is not offered to other people.

What it does

  • Answers spoken and typed requests, opens apps and websites, sets timers and reminders, and plays music.
  • Connects to the owner's Google Calendar to read upcoming events and to add events when asked.
  • Connects to the owner's YouTube account, read-only, to find the owner's playlists so it can play them on request.

Google access

Habibi asks Google for two permissions, both granted by the account owner on Google's own consent screen: Google Calendar events (calendar.events) and read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly). Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
