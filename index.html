About

Built by a tradesman.

I'm Nathan. I come from the trades here in Santa Clarita, so I know the job: one phone call can be a few hundred dollars, and you can't answer it from under a sink.

I started Measured Twice Digital to do for other trades what most of them never have time to do for themselves: be easy to find, look like the real thing, and have proof on Google. I take on one new company at a time, and you'll deal with me directly, not a call center.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website, Google listing and ad accounts are registered in your name and you get the logins. If you ever want to leave, I'll help you move everything.

How fast can I be live?

A first draft of the website takes about 5 business days, and it's live about 7 days after I have your photos and info. It's roughly 20 minutes of your time.

Is there a contract?

Websites have a one-page agreement and a 50% deposit. Monthly plans are month-to-month: cancel any time.

Who pays for ads?

You do, straight to Google, on your own card. My fee is separate and I never touch your ad money.

Can you get me to #1 on Google?

No one honestly can. What I do is set up the basics correctly: the right words, your service area, your listing, a fast mobile site and reviews. That gives you a real chance.

What if I already have a website?

Then we start with what's working and fix what isn't. I'll tell you plainly if your current site is fine and what's worth changing.

What does "one company at a time" mean?

I take on one new company at a time and get it launched and working before I start the next. So you get my full attention. If I'm busy when you call, I'll tell you honestly how long the wait is, and you go next in line.

Which trades do you work with?

Plumbers, electricians, painters and landscapers in Santa Clarita first. Other local trades are welcome: just ask.
